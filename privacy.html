Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Ledger QBO Viewer ("the App") is a private, internal tool used solely by its owner to view data from a single connected QuickBooks Online company. The App is not distributed or made available to the public, and no other individuals or organizations use it. OPT-IN LINK: https://ykroitoro.github.io/mytech-whatsapp-optin/

What data the App accesses

The App connects to QuickBooks Online via the Intuit API and reads the following data, read-only:

  • Customers, Vendors
  • Estimates and Invoices
  • Purchase Orders
  • Company profile information (name and address, used only to label printed documents)

The App does not create, modify, or delete any data in QuickBooks Online.

Where data is stored

The App runs entirely on the owner's own local computer. QuickBooks data is fetched on demand and displayed in the browser; it is not stored in any database. OAuth authorization tokens are stored in a single local file on the same computer, used only to maintain the connection to QuickBooks Online.

Data sharing

No data is shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party. Data flows only between QuickBooks Online and the owner's local computer.

Data retention

Disconnecting the App (or deleting the local token file) immediately revokes its access and removes the stored authorization token. No QuickBooks data is retained by the App outside of an active session in the browser.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the App's owner.
