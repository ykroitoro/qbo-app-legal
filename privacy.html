Privacy Policy — My Tech, LLC

Last updated: September 2026

My Tech, LLC ("My Tech," "we," "us," or "our") is a wholesale distributor of computer hardware and accessories based in Doral, FL. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with (1) our internal QuickBooks Online management tool, and (2) our SMS/WhatsApp customer messaging program.

QuickBooks Online integration

My Tech, LLC operates a private, internal tool used solely by our own staff to view and manage data from our own connected QuickBooks Online company. This tool is not distributed to or used by the public or any other organization. It connects to QuickBooks Online via the Intuit API and reads, on a read-only basis:

  • Customers and Vendors
  • Estimates and Invoices
  • Purchase Orders
  • Company profile information (name and address, used only to label printed documents)

This tool does not create, modify, or delete any data in QuickBooks Online. It runs on My Tech, LLC's own infrastructure; QuickBooks data is fetched on demand and is not stored in any separate database. OAuth authorization tokens are stored securely and used only to maintain the connection to QuickBooks Online.

SMS/WhatsApp messaging program

My Tech, LLC also operates an SMS/WhatsApp messaging program for existing customers and vendors, covering order confirmations, invoices, estimates, purchase order updates, and shipment or backorder status. Phone numbers are only added to this program with the customer's or vendor's direct verbal consent. Details on how to opt in and opt out are described on our opt-in consent page and our Terms & Conditions page.

Data sharing

My Tech, LLC does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Data is used solely to operate our internal QuickBooks tool and our messaging program described above.

Data retention

Disconnecting our QuickBooks integration immediately revokes its access and removes the stored authorization token. Phone numbers in the messaging program are retained only while you remain an active customer or vendor, or until you opt out by replying STOP.
